# Design direction

This portfolio presents Ferdy Kurnia Panggabean as a practical software and systems engineer. Its identity comes from real project work, infrastructure experience, and a direct personal voice.

## Visual direction

- Use warm paper, near-black ink, and vermilion as the single accent. Keep the palette quiet so the work and portrait carry the page.
- Use an editorial rhythm: large type, open margins, and section compositions that follow their content. Prefer project rows and lists over repeated card grids.
- Keep Space Grotesk for display headings and Inter for reading. Use IBM Plex Mono sparingly for metadata that benefits from a technical register.
- Treat the vermilion accent as a navigation and emphasis signal. Avoid blue-purple glow, decorative grids, layered blur, and ornamental effects without a specific content purpose.
- Default to the warm paper theme for long-form reading. Keep the ink theme as a visitor-controlled alternative and remember the choice.
- Use the existing real portrait and project screenshots. Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, statistics, or people.

## Liveliness dials

- ENERGY: 2. Confident and focused, with a strong typographic focal point.
- RHYTHM: 2. Alternate wide project imagery, concise descriptions, and text-led sections without forcing a template.
- MOTION: 1. Keep motion brief and limited to feedback or revealing content. No continuous ambient animation.
